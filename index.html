How to play

  • Build eight runs from King down to Ace, each in a single suit. A finished run leaves the table.
  • Place a card on any card one rank higher, whatever its suit.
  • Several cards can move together only if they run down in order and share a suit.
  • Any card or run can go into an empty column.
  • Tap the stock (top right) to deal one card onto every column. Normally each column needs at least one card first; you can change this in Settings.
  • Tap a card to move it to the best spot. Drag to choose where it goes.
  • Pick 1, 2 or 4 suits under New game. 1 suit is the easiest.
