Spider – Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Spider is made by Meyzenworks. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Games are played entirely on your device. Your progress, settings, scores and statistics are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app needs an internet connection only to download it the first time and to receive updates. The server that delivers those files may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and maintenance; these are not used to identify you.
  • You can delete all saved data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
